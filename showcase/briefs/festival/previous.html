The shipyard

Low Tide takes over a decommissioned shipyard in Gdańsk: concrete quays, a launch slipway that runs down to the water and a hull hall big enough to dance in.

Slipway

14:00–23:00

Open air, on the old launch ramp, facing the water. Daytime and sunset sets.

Hall K

18:00–04:00 Sat · 18:00–02:00 Sun

A former hull assembly hall. Indoors, so it runs whatever the weather.

Getting there

Train
Long-distance trains arrive at Gdańsk Główny. Local SKM trains stop at Gdańsk Stocznia, the closest station to the shipyard.
Plane
Gdańsk Lech Wałęsa Airport (GDN), then train or taxi into the city.
Bike
Staffed bike parking next to the main gate.
Car
There is no festival parking. Please come by public transport.
Late
When Hall K closes, the taxi rank is outside the main gate. Check night bus routes with ZTM Gdańsk before you travel.

FAQ

The practical questions. If yours isn't here, it will be answered with the final set times.

Is Low Tide 18+?

Yes. Everyone needs photo ID at the gate, even with a valid ticket. A passport, national ID card or driving licence all work.

Can I leave and come back in?

Yes, until 22:00 each day. After 22:00, leaving the site ends your night.

What happens if it rains?

Slipway is open air and plays through rain; we pause it if there is lightning nearby. Hall K is indoors and isn't affected.

Is there camping?

No. The festival is in the city, so stay in Gdańsk, Sopot or Gdynia and come in by train.

Can I bring water?

Bring an empty refillable bottle, no glass. There are free water points by both stages.

Can I transfer or refund a ticket?

You can move a ticket to someone else's name for free until 7 August 2027. Tickets are refunded only if the festival is cancelled.

Is the site step-free?

The routes between the gate, both stages, the bars and the toilets are paved and step-free, and there are accessible toilets at both stages. If you need a companion to attend, their ticket is free.

Which day pass should I buy?

Saturday runs later (Hall K until 04:00). Sunday finishes at 02:00. Both days have the same two stages and the same gate time.
