Create a responsive website for "Kowalski Heating & Plumbing", a two-person heating and plumbing business serving Leeds and the surrounding area.

Deliver a single self-contained HTML file (index.html) with inline CSS and JavaScript. It must work without external assets, fonts, or dependencies. Use inline SVG or CSS placeholders instead of external images.

The site must include:
- clear services (boiler servicing and repair, central heating installation, general plumbing, emergency callouts) with what each includes;
- the service area, opening hours, emergency availability, and a phone number (use a clearly fictional number);
- how a job works from first call to completion;
- pricing signals (call-out fee, hourly rate, or fixed-price examples, fictional but plausible);
- credentials (use placeholder registration numbers clearly marked as examples);
- a quote request form (name, phone, postcode, problem description) with validation and a confirmation state;
- a footer with contact and privacy links.

Do not invent customer reviews, ratings, "happy customers" counts, or awards.
