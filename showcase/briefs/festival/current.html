The shipyard

A decommissioned shipyard on the water in Gdańsk. The Slipway stage stands where hulls were launched; the Plate Hall is the old steel-cutting shed.

Getting there

  • On foot

    The shipyard sits on the edge of the city centre. From Gdańsk Główny station, follow the yellow signs to the festival gate.

  • Train

    Gdańsk Główny is served by intercity trains and the SKM city rail. Trains from Gdańsk airport reach the city with one change.

  • Tram & bus

    Several tram lines stop near the main station. Check ZTM Gdańsk for night services after the Plate Hall closes.

  • Bike

    Bike parking inside the gate. There is no car park for the festival.

FAQ

What age do I need to be?

Low Tide is 18+. Bring photo ID; it is checked at the gate together with your ticket.

Can I leave and come back?

Yes, on the same day. Your wristband is scanned on the way out and back in.

What happens if it rains?

The Slipway is open air and plays in light rain. If the weather makes the stage unsafe, sets move into the Plate Hall where possible. Bring a waterproof layer: it is the Baltic coast.

Is the site accessible?

The ground is old concrete and steel plate, mostly level. There is a step-free route from the gate to both stages, a viewing platform at the Slipway and accessible toilets. Write to the access team before the festival if you need a companion ticket.

Can I bring food and drink?

Sealed water bottles up to 0.5 l are fine. Free water refill points are next to both stages. Other food and drink is sold on site.

Can I get a refund?

Tickets are not refundable, but you can transfer a ticket to someone else's name until 7 August 2027.
