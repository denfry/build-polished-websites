Practice areas

Four kinds of work, all of it family law

Divorce

Oregon courts call it dissolution of marriage. We take you through filing, dividing property and debt, spousal support and, where children are involved, the parenting plan.

  • Uncontested and contested dissolutions
  • Property, debt and retirement division
  • Spousal support

Custody & parenting time

Decisions about where children live and how time is shared. We help you build a workable parenting plan, and represent you in court when agreement isn't possible.

  • Parenting plans and schedules
  • Custody and parenting-time disputes
  • Modifying existing orders

Mediation

Both people sit down with a neutral mediator to reach their own agreement, usually faster and at lower cost than going to trial. Mateo Reyes mediates in English and Spanish.

  • Divorce and parenting-plan mediation
  • Reviewing agreements reached elsewhere
  • Representation in court-ordered mediation

Prenuptial agreements

Agreements made before marriage (Oregon law calls them premarital agreements) about property, debts and support. We draft them, or review one your partner's lawyer has drafted.

  • Drafting and negotiation
  • Independent review for one partner
  • Postnuptial agreements

We don't handle criminal defense, immigration or bankruptcy. If your situation involves one of these, we'll say so in the first conversation and suggest where to look.

First consultation

How the free 30-minute consultation works

  1. You send a request

    Use the form below or call. We ask for the other person's name so we can run a conflict check: we can't advise both people in the same family matter.

  2. We reply within one business day

    A member of our office contacts you to set a time: at our office on Hawthorne, by video, or by phone. Evening video slots are available on Tuesdays and Thursdays.

  3. The 30 minutes

    You tell us what's going on. A partner explains how the Oregon process works for your situation, the likely steps, a realistic sense of timing, and how fees would work. You can ask anything.

  4. Afterwards, you decide

    There's no pressure to hire us. If you want to go ahead, we send a written engagement letter that sets out the scope of work and fees before any work starts.

Useful to have with you

  • Any court papers you've received or filed
  • Existing orders, parenting plans or agreements
  • A rough list of major assets and debts
  • Your questions, written down

What 30 minutes can't cover

  • A full review of documents
  • Drafting or filing anything
  • Representation; that starts only after a signed engagement letter
