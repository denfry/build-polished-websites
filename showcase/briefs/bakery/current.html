The oven came first

Forno Alto is built around one wood-fired oven at the back of a narrow shop in Bonfim. The fire is lit before dawn, and everything on the shelves is baked in it that same morning.

Our breads are leavened with a long-kept sourdough starter and given a slow, cool rise overnight. That makes a deep crust, an open crumb and bread that keeps for days.

We bake a set amount each morning. When it is gone, we close, which is why we take reservations: a loaf with your name on it is set aside before the door opens.

  1. before dawnFire lit, oven left to soak up the heat
  2. early morningEmbers raked out, first loaves on the peel
  3. 7:30Door opens; pastries and coffee
  4. 14:00Closing time, or earlier if we sell out
