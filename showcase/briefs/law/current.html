What we help with

Four kinds of family matter, all under Oregon law. If yours is not listed, ask in the consultation and we will tell you honestly whether we are the right fit.

Divorce

Dissolution of marriage in Oregon

Whether you and your spouse agree on most things or on very little, we explain the process from filing to judgment, and what each step asks of you.

  • Dividing property, debts and retirement accounts
  • Spousal support
  • Uncontested divorces handled through agreed paperwork
  • Contested cases taken to court when agreement is not possible

Custody & parenting time

Parenting plans, custody, child support

A parenting plan sets out where children live and when. We help you build one that fits your children's school weeks, your work and the distance between homes, and we help change one when life changes.

Mediation

Reaching agreement outside court

In mediation, both of you sit down with a neutral mediator to work out property, support or parenting arrangements yourselves. We act as mediator for couples, or advise one of you while you mediate elsewhere, never both at once.

Mediation is not right for every family. Where there has been abuse or one person feels unable to speak freely, we will say so and suggest another route.

Prenuptial agreements

Before marriage, or after it

A prenuptial agreement records how you will handle property, debts and support if the marriage ends. We draft agreements, review ones drafted by the other side, and explain each clause in plain English.

Give yourselves time: an agreement signed in a hurry, close to the wedding, is easier to challenge later.

How we charge

Family matters are stressful enough without surprise invoices. You will know how a matter is charged before any work begins.

First consultation

Free, 30 minutes, with a partner.

Fixed fees

For work with a predictable shape, such as an uncontested divorce or drafting a prenuptial agreement, we quote one fixed price in writing.

Hourly, with estimates

Contested matters are billed hourly against a retainer. We give you a written estimate for each stage, and tell you before an estimate is exceeded.

Mediation

Billed hourly and usually shared between both people, in whatever proportion you agree.

Monthly statements

Itemised, in plain language: who did what, for how long, and why.
